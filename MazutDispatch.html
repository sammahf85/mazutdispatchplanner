Depots

DepotTrucks

Each truck loads at and returns to its home depot. Each order is served from the client's chosen depot, or automatically from the nearest depot. When a depot has no orders left, its free trucks help the other depots.

If the orders are more than the trucks can carry at once, the planner schedules extra trips: whichever truck gets back to its depot first takes the next load, so every driver's next trip is ready when they return. Click 🚚 Start trip when a truck leaves and ✅ Trip completed when it is back – delivered quantities are taken off the orders. New orders during the day? Add them and click Generate again (with Now) – completed trips and trucks on the road are kept.

📡 Live driver locations

TruckWhere / when🔋

Each driver's phone sends its GPS position with the free OwnTracks app (works in the background, screen off). Click 📲 Set up phone next to a truck once to connect that driver's phone. Positions are encrypted with your company's secret key – only this program can read them. Tell your drivers their phone location is shared during work.

⚙ Tracking connection settings (normally no need to change)

Uses the free public HiveMQ relay by default. For a private relay, create a free broker (e.g. HiveMQ Cloud) and enter it here with its username/password. After changing the code, key or server, set up the phones again.

Data is saved automatically on this computer (inside this browser). Use backups to move data to another PC.

Automatic daily backup (Excel)

Every day at 7:00 pm Lebanon time the program saves an Excel file (orders, clients, trucks, depots, trips, settings) into the backup folder on the Desktop of this computer. Choose the folder once on each computer. The program must be open at 7 pm; if it was closed, the backup is made the next time you open it.
